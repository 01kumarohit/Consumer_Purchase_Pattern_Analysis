# Consumer Purchase Pattern Analysis

An end-to-end analysis of retail consumer shopping behavior — combining SQL-based data exploration, Python EDA, and an interactive Power BI dashboard — to help a retail company understand what drives customer purchases, engagement, and loyalty.

## 📌 Problem Statement

A leading retail company wants to better understand its customers' shopping behavior in order to improve sales, customer satisfaction, and long-term loyalty. Management has noticed shifting purchasing patterns across demographics, product categories, and sales channels (online vs. offline), and wants to know which factors — discounts, reviews, seasonality, or payment preferences — drive consumer decisions and repeat purchases.

**Business Question:**
> How can the company leverage consumer shopping data to identify trends, improve customer engagement, and optimize marketing and product strategies?

## 🔍 Key Business Questions Answered (SQL)

1. Total revenue generated by male vs. female customers
2. Customers who used a discount but still spent above the average purchase amount
3. Top 5 products with the highest average review rating
4. Average purchase amount: Standard vs. Express shipping
5. Do subscribed customers spend more? (avg. spend & total revenue comparison)
6. Top 5 products with the highest percentage of discounted purchases
7. Customer segmentation into New, Returning, and Loyal based on purchase history
8. Top 3 most purchased products within each category
9. Are repeat buyers (5+ previous purchases) more likely to subscribe?
10. Revenue contribution by age group

See [`sqlques.sql`](./sqlques.sql) for the full queries.

## 📊 Power BI Dashboard

The dashboard provides an interactive view of consumer purchase patterns, including:

- **KPIs:** Number of customers, average purchase amount, average review rating
- **Subscription breakdown:** % of customers by subscription status
- **Category performance:** Revenue and sales by product category (Clothing, Accessories, Footwear, Outerwear)
- **Age group insights:** Sales and revenue by age group (Young Adult, Middle Age, Senior, Adult)
- **Filters:** Subscription status, gender, category, and shipping type

## 💡 Key Insights

- Revenue and customer engagement vary meaningfully across demographics, product categories, and shipping preferences.
- Discount usage and review ratings both play a role in shaping purchase behavior and product popularity.
- Customer segmentation (New / Returning / Loyal) highlights opportunities for targeted retention strategies.
- Subscription status correlates with purchasing behavior, offering insight into loyalty program effectiveness.
